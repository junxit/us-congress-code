# § 61e. Transferred

## Notes

### Codification

Section 61e was editorially reclassified as section 6591 of this title.

### Prior Provisions

A prior section 61e, act Aug. 5, 1955, ch. 568, § 1, 69 Stat. 501, prescribed gross annual compensation of Sergeant at Arms of Senate.
