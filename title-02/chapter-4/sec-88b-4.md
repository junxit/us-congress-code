# § 88b–4. Regulations of Page Board

The Page Board shall have authority to prescribe such regulations as may be necessary to carry out sections 88b–2 to 88b–4 of this title.

## Source credit

(Pub. L. 97–377, title I, § 127, Dec. 21, 1982, 96 Stat. 1914.)

## Notes

### Codification

Section is based on section 3 of House Resolution No. 611, Ninety-seventh Congress, Nov. 30, 1982, which was enacted into permanent law by Pub. L. 97–377.
