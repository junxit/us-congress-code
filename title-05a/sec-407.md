# § 407. Omitted]

## Notes

### Editorial Notes

### Codification

Section, Pub. L. 95–521, title IV, § 407, as added Pub. L. 98–150, § 4, Nov. 11, 1983, 97 Stat. 960; amended Pub. L. 100–598, § 8, Nov. 3, 1988, 102 Stat. 3035, amended sections 5314 and 5316 of Title 5, Government Organization and Employees. Prior to amendment by Pub. L. 100–598, section required the President to include estimated expenditures and proposed appropriations for the Office of Government Ethics in the budget submitted to Congress.
