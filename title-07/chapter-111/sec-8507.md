# § 8507. Miscellaneous matters

- **(a)** *Availability of appropriated funds* Amounts appropriated under this chapter shall remain available until expended.
- **(b)** *Administrative expenses* Of the amounts appropriated to carry out this chapter for a fiscal year, the Secretaries may expend not more than five percent to cover the administrative expenses necessary to carry out this chapter.
