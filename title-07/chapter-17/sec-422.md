# § 422. Omitted

## Notes

### Codification

Section, act May 29, 1928, ch. 892, § 1, 45 Stat. 981, directed Secretary of Agriculture to establish a dairying station in Lewisburg, Tennessee, and was omitted from the Code as obsolete.
