# § 949. Telephone bank receipts; availability for obligations and expenditures

Any receipts from the activities of the telephone bank shall be available for all obligations and expenditures of the telephone bank.

## Source credit

(May 20, 1936, ch. 432, title IV, § 409, as added Pub. L. 92–12, § 2, May 7, 1971, 85 Stat. 36.)

## Notes

### Effective Date

Section effective May 7, 1971, see section 7 of Pub. L. 92–12, set out as a note under section 921a of this title.
