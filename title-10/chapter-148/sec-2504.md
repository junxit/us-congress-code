# § 2504. Renumbered § 4814]

## Notes

### Editorial Notes

### Prior Provisions

A prior section 2504, added Pub. L. 102–484, div. D, title XLII, § 4214(a), Oct. 23, 1992, 106 Stat. 2666, established Center for Study of Defense Economic Adjustment, prior to repeal by Pub. L. 103–160, div. A, title XIII, § 1312(a)(1), Nov. 30, 1993, 107 Stat. 1786. Another prior section 2504 was renumbered section 4851 of this title.
