# § 1321. Filing of plan

The debtor shall file a plan.

## Source credit

(Pub. L. 95–598, Nov. 6, 1978, 92 Stat. 2648.)

## Notes

### Historical and Revision Notes

### senate report no. 95–989

Chapter 13 contemplates the filing of a plan only by the debtor.
