# § 9521. Definition

In this part, the term “MBDA Business Center Program” means the program established under section ___113. 11 So in original. See References in Text note below.

### References in Text

Section ___113, referred to in text, probably means section 100113 of Pub. L. 117–58, which is classified to section 9523 of this title.
