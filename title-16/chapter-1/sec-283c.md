# § 283c. Administration

The Guadalupe Mountains National Park shall be administered by the Secretary of the Interior in accordance with the provisions of sections 1, 2, 3, and 4 11 See References in Text note below. of this title, as amended and supplemented.

## Source credit

(Pub. L. 89–667, § 4, Oct. 15, 1966, 80 Stat. 922.)

## Notes

1 See References in Text note below.

### References in Text

Sections 1, 2, 3, and 4 of this title, referred to in text, were repealed by Pub. L. 113–287, § 7, Dec. 19, 2014, 128 Stat. 3272. For disposition of sections, see Disposition Table preceding section 100101 of Title 54, National Park Service and Related Programs.
