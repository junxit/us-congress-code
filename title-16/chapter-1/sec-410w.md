# § 410w. Administration, protection, and development

When established pursuant to this subchapter, the park shall be administered, protected, and developed by the Secretary of the Interior in accordance with the provisions of sections 1, 2, 3, and 4 11 See References in Text note below. of this title, as amended and supplemented, and sections 461 to 467 1 of this title.
