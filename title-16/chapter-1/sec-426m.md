# § 426m. Administration, protection, and development

The administration, protection and development of the Stones River National Battlefield shall be exercised by the Secretary of the Interior in accordance with the provisions of sections 1, 2, 3, and 4 11 See References in Text note below. of this title, as amended.

## Source credit

(Pub. L. 86–443, § 3, Apr. 22, 1960, 74 Stat. 82.)

## Notes

1 See References in Text note below.

### References in Text

Sections 1, 2, 3, and 4 of this title, referred to in text, were repealed by Pub. L. 113–287, § 7, Dec. 19, 2014, 128 Stat. 3272. For disposition of sections, see Disposition Table preceding section 100101 of Title 54, National Park Service and Related Programs.
