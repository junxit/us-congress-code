# § 450b to 450e. Repealed. Pub. L. 94–578, title III, § 308(e), Oct. 21, 1976, 90 Stat. 2736

## Notes

### removalDescription

Section 450b, acts June 18, 1930, ch. 520, § 1, 46 Stat. 777; Aug. 13, 1935, ch. 520, § 1, 49 Stat. 613; Apr. 15, 1954, ch. 142, 68 Stat. 54, provided for creation of the Appomattox Court House National Historical Park.

### removalDescription

Section 450c, acts June 18, 1930, ch. 520, § 2, 46 Stat. 777; Aug. 13, 1935, ch. 520, § 1, 49 Stat. 613, authorized appropriation of $100,000 for the Appomattox Court House National Historical Park.

### removalDescription

Section 450d, acts June 18, 1930, ch. 520, § 3, 46 Stat. 777; Aug. 13, 1935, ch. 520, § 1, 49 Stat. 613, authorized Secretary of the Interior to accept donations of land or buildings within boundaries of the park.

### removalDescription

Section 450d–1, acts July 17, 1953, ch. 227, 67 Stat. 181; Apr. 15, 1954, ch. 142, 68 Stat. 54, authorized exchange of land in park for adjacent non-Federal land.

### removalDescription

Section 450e, act June 18, 1930, ch. 520, § 4, as added Aug. 13, 1935, ch. 520, § 2, 49 Stat. 614; amended Apr. 15, 1954, ch. 142, 68 Stat. 54, provided for administration of park by National Park Service under the direction of Secretary of the Interior.
