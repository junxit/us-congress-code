# § 450f to 450k. Repealed. Dec. 21, 1944, ch. 634, § 1, 58 Stat. 852

## Notes

### removalDescription

Section 450f, act Aug. 15, 1935, ch. 547, § 1, 49 Stat. 652, related to establishment of Patrick Henry National Monument.

### removalDescription

Sections 450f–1 and 450f–2, act Jan. 29, 1940, ch. 16, 54 Stat. 18, related to acquisition of Patrick Henry’s estate and erection of a permanent public memorial.

### removalDescription

Sections 450g to 450k, act Aug. 15, 1935, ch. 547, §§ 2–6, 49 Stat. 652, 653, related to administration, etc., of Monument.

### Unexpended Funds

Section 2 of act Dec. 21, 1944, ch. 634, 58 Stat. 853, provided that all unexpended balances of amounts appropriated were to be covered into the surplus fund of the Treasury.
