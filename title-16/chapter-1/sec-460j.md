# § 460j. Disposition of proceeds

The proceeds from any sale made under this subchapter shall be covered into the Treasury of the United States as miscellaneous receipts.

## Source credit

(Aug. 6, 1956, ch. 987, § 6, 70 Stat. 1066.)
