# § 460m–12. Administration, protection, and development

The Secretary shall administer, protect, and develop the Buffalo National River in accordance with the provisions of sections 1, 2, 3, and 4 11 See References in Text note below. of this title, as amended and supplemented; except that any other statutory authority available to the Secretary for the conservation and management of natural resources may be utilized to the extent he finds such authority will further the purposes of this subchapter.

## Source credit

(Pub. L. 92–237, § 5, Mar. 1, 1972, 86 Stat. 45.)

### References in Text

Sections 1, 2, 3, and 4 of this title, referred to in text, were repealed by Pub. L. 113–287, § 7, Dec. 19, 2014, 128 Stat. 3272. For disposition of sections, see Disposition Table preceding section 100101 of Title 54, National Park Service and Related Programs.
