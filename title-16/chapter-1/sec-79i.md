# § 79i. Administration

The Secretary shall administer the Redwood National Park in accordance with the provisions of sections 1, 2, 3, and 4 11 See References in Text note below. of this title, as amended and supplemented.

(Pub. L. 90–545, § 9, Oct. 2, 1968, 82 Stat. 934.)
