# § 690h. “Person” defined

As used in sections 690 to 690i of this title the term “person” includes an individual, partnership, association, or corporation.

## Source credit

(Apr. 23, 1928, ch. 413, § 10, 45 Stat. 450.)

## Notes

### References in Text
