# § 4728. Brown tree snake control program

The Task Force shall, within the program developed under subsection (a),11 So in original. Probably should be “subsection (a) of section 4722 of this title,”. undertake a comprehensive, environmentally sound program in coordination with regional, territorial, State and local entities to control the brown tree snake (Boiga irregularis) in Guam and other areas where the species is established outside of its historic range.
