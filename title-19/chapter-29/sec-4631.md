# § 4631. Definitions

In this subchapter:

- **(1)** *Labor attaché* The term “labor attaché” means an individual hired under part B.
- **(2)** *Labor obligations* The term “labor obligations” means the obligations under chapter 23 of the USMCA (relating to labor).
- **(3)** *Mexico’s labor reform* The term “Mexico’s labor reform” means the legislation on labor reform enacted by Mexico on May 1, 2019.
