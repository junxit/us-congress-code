# § 1653a. Transferred

## Notes

### Codification

Section, act June 25, 1938, ch. 679, § 37, 52 Stat. 1094, related to the effective date of the Customs Administrative Act of 1938, and is set out as a note under section 1401 of this title. Section was not part of Tariff Act of 1930 which constitutes this chapter.
