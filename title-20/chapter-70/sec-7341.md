# § 7341. Short title

This part may be cited as the “Rural Education Achievement Program”.

## Source credit

(Pub. L. 89–10, title VI, § 6201, as added Pub. L. 107–110, title VI, § 601, Jan. 8, 2002, 115 Stat. 1891.)

## Notes

### Prior Provisions

A prior section 6201 of Pub. L. 89–10 was classified to section 7331 of this title, prior to the general amendment of this subchapter by Pub. L. 107–110. Another prior section 6201 of Pub. L. 89–10 was classified to section 3271 of this title, prior to the general amendment of Pub. L. 89–10 by Pub. L. 103–382.
