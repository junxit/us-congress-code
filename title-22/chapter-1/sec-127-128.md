# § 127, 128. Transferred

## Notes

### Editorial Notes

### Codification

Section 127, R.S. § 1745, which related to regulation of fees by President, was transferred to section 4219 of this title. Section 128, R.S. § 1746, which related to medium for payment of fees, was transferred to section 4220 of this title.
