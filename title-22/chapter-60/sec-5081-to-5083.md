# § 5081 to 5083. Repealed. Pub. L. 103–149, § 4(a)(1), Nov. 23, 1993, 107 Stat. 1504

## Notes

### removalDescription

### removalDescription

Section 5082, Pub. L. 99–440, title IV, § 402, Oct. 2, 1986, 100 Stat. 1107; Pub. L. 99–631, § 1(a)(25), Nov. 7, 1986, 100 Stat. 3518, authorized President to limit imports from foreign countries benefitting from sanctions or prohibitions imposed under this chapter.

### removalDescription

Section 5083, Pub. L. 99–440, title IV, § 403, Oct. 2, 1986, 100 Stat. 1107, related to private right of action.
