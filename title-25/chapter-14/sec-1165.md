# § 1165. Tax exemption

No part of any funds distributed or held in trust under the provisions of this subchapter shall be subject to Federal or State income taxes.

## Source credit

(Pub. L. 90–117, § 5, Oct. 31, 1967, 81 Stat. 338.)
