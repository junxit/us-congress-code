# § 1253. Omitted

## Notes

### Editorial Notes

### Codification

Section, Pub. L. 92–253, § 3, Mar. 17, 1972, 86 Stat. 64, which related to payments to minors and persons under a legal disability, was omitted from the Code as being of special and not general application.
