# § 1300b–3. Omitted

## Notes

### Editorial Notes

### Codification

Section, Pub. L. 92–467, § 4, Oct. 6, 1972, 86 Stat. 781, which related to payments to minors and persons under legal disability, was omitted from the Code as being of special and not general application.
