# § 506. Omitted

## Notes

### Codification

Section, act June 26, 1936, ch. 831, § 6, 49 Stat. 1968, which authorized the Secretary of the Interior to make loans to individual Indians and to associations or corporate groups organized pursuant to this subchapter and appropriated funds for such purpose, was omitted from the Code as being of special and not general application.
