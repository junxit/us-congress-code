# § 507. Omitted

## Notes

### removalDescription

Section, act June 26, 1936, ch. 831, § 7, 49 Stat. 1968, which related to availability and allocation of funds and treatment of royalties derived from mineral deposits underlying lands purchased in Oklahoma, was omitted from the Code as being of special and not general application.
