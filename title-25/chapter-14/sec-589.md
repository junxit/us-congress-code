# § 589. Tax exemption

Any funds distributed per capita under provisions of sections 581 to 590 of this title shall not be subject to Federal or State income tax.

## Source credit

(Pub. L. 92–206, § 9, Dec. 18, 1971, 85 Stat. 739.)
