# § 589. Omitted

## Notes

### Editorial Notes

### Codification

Section, Pub. L. 92–206, § 9, Dec. 18, 1971, 85 Stat. 739, which exempted distributed funds from Federal and State income taxes, was omitted from the Code as being of special and not general application.
