# § 623. Omitted

## Notes

### Editorial Notes

### Codification

Section, act Aug. 13, 1949, ch. 425, § 3, 63 Stat. 605, which related to disbursement of deposits in the United Pueblos Agency, was omitted from the Code as being of special and not general application.
