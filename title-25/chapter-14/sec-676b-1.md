# § 676b–1. Omitted

## Notes

### Editorial Notes

### Codification

Section, Pub. L. 91–420, § 2, Sept. 25, 1970, 84 Stat. 871, which exempted distributed funds from Federal and State income taxes, was omitted from the Code as being of special and not general application.
