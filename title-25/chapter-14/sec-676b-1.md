# § 676b–1. Tax exemption

Any portion of the funds distributed per capita to the members of the respective tribes shall not be subject to Federal or State income tax.

## Source credit

(Pub. L. 91–420, § 2, Sept. 25, 1970, 84 Stat. 871.)
