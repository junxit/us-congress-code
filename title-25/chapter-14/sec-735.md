# § 735. Adoption of new constitution and bylaws

Upon written request of the tribal council,11 So in original. Probably should be “Tribal Council,”. the Secretary shall hold an election for the members of the tribe for the purpose of adopting a new constitution and bylaws in accordance with section 476 of this title.

## Source credit

(Pub. L. 100–89, title II, § 205, Aug. 18, 1987, 101 Stat. 671.)
