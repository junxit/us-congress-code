# § 881. Omitted

## Notes

### Editorial Notes

### Codification

Section, Pub. L. 91–401, § 1, Sept. 16, 1970, 84 Stat. 838, which related to disposition of the judgment fund of the Potawatomi Indians, was omitted from the Code as being of special and not general application.
