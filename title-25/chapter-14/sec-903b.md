# § 903b. Omitted

## Notes

### Codification

Section, Pub. L. 93–197, § 4, Dec. 22, 1973, 87 Stat. 771, which set out provisions relating to the Menominee Restoration Committee, was omitted from the Code as being of special and not general application.
