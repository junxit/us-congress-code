# § 994. Tax exemption

No part of any funds which may be distributed in accordance with the provisions of this subchapter shall be subject to Federal or State income tax.

## Source credit

(Pub. L. 87–775, § 4, Oct. 9, 1962, 76 Stat. 776.)
