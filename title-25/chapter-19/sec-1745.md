# § 1745. Omitted

## Notes

### Editorial Notes

### Codification

Section, Pub. L. 97–399, § 6, Dec. 31, 1982, 96 Stat. 2014, which provided for special provisions for the Miccosukee Tribe, was omitted from the Code as being of special and not general application.
