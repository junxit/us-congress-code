# § 1753. Omitted

## Notes

### Editorial Notes

### Codification

Section, Pub. L. 98–134, § 4, Oct. 18, 1983, 97 Stat. 852, which related to extinguishment of aboriginal titles and Indian claims, was omitted from the Code as being of special and not general application.
