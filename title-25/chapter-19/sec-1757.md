# § 1757. Omitted

## Notes

### Editorial Notes

### Codification

Section, Pub. L. 98–134, § 8, Oct. 18, 1983, 97 Stat. 855, which provided for a restriction against alienation of lands, was omitted from the Code as being of special and not general application.
