# § 127. Repealed. Pub. L. 117–317, § 2(2), Dec. 27, 2022, 136 Stat. 4419

## Notes

### removalDescription

Section, R.S. § 2100; May 29, 1928, ch. 901, § 1(81), 45 Stat. 992, disallowed payment to hostile Indian tribes of moneys or annuities stipulated by a treaty.
