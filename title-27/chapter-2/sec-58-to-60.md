# § 58 to 60. Repealed. Mar. 22, 1933, ch. 4, § 2, 48 Stat. 17

## Notes

### removalDescription

Section 58, act Oct. 28, 1919, ch. 85, title II, § 37, 41 Stat. 318, related to development of liquids containing less than one-half of one per centum of alcohol.

### removalDescription

Section 59, act Oct. 28, 1919, ch. 85, title II, § 37, 41 Stat. 318, related to tax on fortified wines for nonbeverage alcohol.

### removalDescription

Section 60, act Oct. 28, 1919, ch. 85, title II, § 37, 41 Stat. 318, related to burden of proof with regard to alcoholic content.
