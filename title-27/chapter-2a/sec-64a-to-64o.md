# § 64a to 64o. Repealed. Aug. 27, 1935, ch. 740, title II, § 202(a), 49 Stat. 877

## Notes

### removalDescription

Section 64a, act Mar. 22, 1933, ch. 4, § 3, 48 Stat. 17, related to manufacture, sale, and transfer of beer, ale, porter, etc., of not more than 3.2 per centum of alcohol.

### removalDescription

Section 64b, act Mar. 22, 1933, ch. 4, § 3(c), 48 Stat. 17, related to use of mails for advertisements of beer, ale, porter, etc., of not more than 3.2 per centum of alcohol.

### removalDescription

Section 64c, act Mar. 22, 1933, ch. 4, § 4, 48 Stat. 17, related to permits to manufacture for sale beer, ale, porter, etc., of not more than 3.2 per centum of alcohol.

### removalDescription

Section 64d, act Mar. 22, 1933, ch. 4, § 4(b), 48 Stat. 18, related to development of beer, ale, porter, etc., of an alcoholic content in excess of that specified in permit with a proviso that such content will subsequently be reduced to prescribed limit.

### removalDescription

Section 64e, act Mar. 22, 1933, ch. 4, § 4(b)(2), 48 Stat. 18, related to exemption from taxation of fortified wines when used for production of non­beverage alcohol.

### removalDescription

Section 64f, act Mar. 22, 1933, ch. 4, § 4(b)(4), 48 Stat. 18, related to burden of proof as to alcoholic content of beer, ale, porter, etc.

### removalDescription

Section 64g, act Mar. 22, 1933, ch. 4, § 4(c), 48 Stat. 18, related to penalties for unlawful manufacture or sale of beer, ale, porter, etc., under this chapter.

### removalDescription

Section 64h, act Mar. 22, 1933, ch. 4, § 4(d), 48 Stat. 19, related to geographical applications of sections 64c to 64g of this title.

### removalDescription

Section 64i, act Mar. 22, 1933, ch. 4, § 5, 48 Stat. 19, related to requirement that at time of sale any beer, ale, porter, etc., manufactured under this chapter must contain no more than 3.2 per centum of alcohol.

### removalDescription

Section 64j, act Mar. 22, 1933, ch. 4, § 6, 48 Stat. 19, related to prohibition of shipment of beer, ale, porter, etc., of an alcoholic content of 3.2 per centum into dry States.

### removalDescription

Section 64k, act Mar. 22, 1933, ch. 4, § 7, 48 Stat. 19, related to penalties for violation of prohibition of shipment of beer, ale, porter, etc., into dry States.

### removalDescription

Section 64l, act Mar. 22, 1933, ch. 4, § 8, 48 Stat. 19, related to penalties and forfeitures incurred prior to effective date of chapter.

### removalDescription

Section 64m, act Mar. 22, 1933, ch. 4, § 1(c), 48 Stat. 16, related to effect of chapter on existing legislation with respect to internal revenue laws.

### removalDescription

Section 64n, act Mar. 22, 1933, ch. 4, § 9, 48 Stat. 19, related to effective date of chapter.

### removalDescription

Section 64o, act Mar. 22, 1933, ch. 4, § 10, 48 Stat. 20, provided for a savings clause for provisions of chapter.
