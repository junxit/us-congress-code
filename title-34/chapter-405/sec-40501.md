# § 40501. Program authorized

The Attorney General is authorized to provide grant awards to States to enable States to improve the reporting of unidentified and missing persons.

## Source credit

(Pub. L. 106–177, title II, § 202, Mar. 10, 2000, 114 Stat. 36.)

## Notes

### Codification

Section was formerly classified to section 14661 of Title 42, The Public Health and Welfare, prior to editorial reclassification and renumbering as this section.
