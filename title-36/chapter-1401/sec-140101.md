# § 140101. Organization

- **(a)** *Federal Charter.—* Marine Corps League (in this chapter, the “corporation”) is a federally chartered corporation.
- **(b)** *Perpetual Existence.—* Except as otherwise provided, the corporation has perpetual existence.
