# § 154101. Organization

- **(a)** *Federal Charter.—* Naval Sea Cadet Corps (in this chapter, the “corporation”) is a federally chartered corporation.
- **(b)** *Perpetual Existence.—* Except as otherwise provided, the corporation has perpetual existence.
