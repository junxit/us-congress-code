# § 9431 to 9438. Repealed. Pub. L. 97–35, title IX, § 902(e)(1), Aug. 13, 1981, 95 Stat. 560

## Notes

### removalDescription

Section 9431, Pub. L. 96–398, title II, § 201, Oct. 7, 1980, 94 Stat. 1571, set forth authority, etc., for grants for community mental health centers.

### removalDescription

Section 9432, Pub. L. 96–398, title II, § 202, Oct. 7, 1980, 94 Stat. 1573, set forth authority, etc., for grants for services for chronically, mentally ill individuals.

### removalDescription

Section 9433, Pub. L. 96–398, title II, § 203, Oct. 7, 1980, 94 Stat. 1575, set forth authority, etc., for grants for services for severely, mentally disturbed children and adults.

### removalDescription

Section 9434, Pub. L. 96–398, title II, § 204, Oct. 7, 1980, 94 Stat. 1577, set forth authority, etc., for grants for mental health services for elderly individuals and other priority populations.

### removalDescription

Section 9435, Pub. L. 96–398, title II, § 205, Oct. 7, 1980, 94 Stat. 1581, set forth authority, etc., for grants for non-revenue producing services.

### removalDescription

Section 9436, Pub. L. 96–398, title II, § 206, Oct. 7, 1980, 94 Stat. 1582, set forth authority, etc., for grants for mental health services in health care centers.

### removalDescription

Section 9437, Pub. L. 96–398, title II, § 207, Oct. 7, 1980, 94 Stat. 1583, set forth authority, etc., for grants and contracts for innovative projects.

### removalDescription

Section 9438, Pub. L. 96–398, title II, § 208, Oct. 7, 1980, 94 Stat. 1584, set forth authority, etc., for grants for prevention of mental illness and promotion of mental health.

### Effective Date of Repeal

Repeal effective Oct. 1, 1981, see section 902(h) of Pub. L. 97–35, set out as an Effective Date of 1981 Amendment note under section 238l of this title.
