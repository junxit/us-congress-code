# § 19531. Severability

If any provision of this Act, or the application thereof to any person or circumstance, is held invalid, the remainder of the Act, and the application of such provisions to other persons or circumstances, shall not be affected thereby.

## Source credit

(Pub. L. 119–101, title XII, § 1201, July 11, 2026, 140 Stat. 984.)

## Notes

### Editorial Notes

### References in Text

This Act, referred to in text, is Pub. L. 119–101, July 11, 2026, 140 Stat. 846, known as the 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act. Titles II, X, and XII of the Act are classified principally to subchapters I, II, and III, respectively, of this chapter. For complete classification of this Act to the Code, see Tables.
