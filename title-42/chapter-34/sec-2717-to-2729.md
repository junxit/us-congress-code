# § 2717 to 2729. Repealed. Pub. L. 93–203, title VI, § 614, Dec. 28, 1973, 87 Stat. 883

## Notes

### removalDescription

Section 2717, Pub. L. 88–452, title I, § 107, Aug. 20, 1964, 78 Stat. 511; Pub. L. 90–222, title I, § 101, Dec. 23, 1967, 81 Stat. 675, provided for establishment of Job Corps centers.

### removalDescription

Section 2718, Pub. L. 88–452, title I, § 108, Aug. 20, 1964, 78 Stat. 511; Pub. L. 90–222, title I, § 101, Dec. 23, 1967, 81 Stat. 676, covered Job Corps program activities.

### removalDescription

Section 2719, Pub. L. 88–452, title I, § 109, Aug. 20, 1964, 78 Stat. 511; Pub. L. 90–222, title I, § 101, Dec. 23, 1967, 81 Stat. 676, covered allowances and support for Job Corps enrollees.

### removalDescription

Section 2720, Pub. L. 88–452, title I, § 110, Aug. 20, 1964, 78 Stat. 511; Pub. L. 89–253, § 7, Oct. 9, 1965, 79 Stat. 974; Pub. L. 90–222, title I, § 101, Dec. 23, 1967, 81 Stat. 677, set out standards of conduct and discipline for Job Corps enrollees.

### removalDescription

Section 2721, Pub. L. 88–452, title I, § 111, as added Pub. L. 89–794, title I, § 110, Nov. 8, 1966, 80 Stat. 1453; amended Pub. L. 90–222, title I, § 101, Dec. 23, 1967, 81 Stat. 677, covered community participation in Job Corps activities.

### removalDescription

Section 2722, Pub. L. 88–452, title I, § 111–1, as added Pub. L. 89–794, title I, § 111, Nov. 8, 1966, 80 Stat. 1454, provided for experimental and demonstration projects and directed Director to report to Congress no later than Mar. 1, 1968.

### removalDescription

Section 2723, Pub. L. 88–452, title I, § 112, as added Pub. L. 90–222, title I, § 101, Dec. 23, 1967, 81 Stat. 678, provided for counseling and job placement.

### removalDescription

Section 2724, Pub. L. 88–452, title I, § 113, as added Pub. L. 90–222, title I, § 101, Dec. 23, 1967, 81 Stat. 679; amended Pub. L. 92–424, § 27(b)(1), Sept. 19, 1972, 86 Stat. 705, covered experimental and development projects.

### removalDescription

Section 2725, Pub. L. 88–452, title I, § 114, as added Pub. L. 90–222, title I, § 101, Dec. 23, 1967, 81 Stat. 680, covered advisory boards and committees.

### removalDescription

Section 2726, Pub. L. 88–452, title I, § 115, as added Pub. L. 90–222, title I, § 101, Dec. 23, 1967, 81 Stat. 680, covered participation of States.

### removalDescription

Section 2727, Pub. L. 88–452, title I, § 116, as added Pub. L. 90–222, title I, § 101, Dec. 23, 1967, 81 Stat. 681; amended Pub. L. 90–623, § 5(a), Oct. 22, 1968, 82 Stat. 1315, covered the application of provisions of Federal law.

### removalDescription

Section 2728, Pub. L. 88–452, title I, § 117, as added Pub. L. 90–222, title I, § 101, Dec. 23, 1967, 81 Stat. 682, set out certain special limitations.

### removalDescription

Section 2729, Pub. L. 88–452, title I, § 118, as added Pub. L. 90–222, title I, § 101, Dec. 23, 1967, 81 Stat. 682, prohibited political discrimination and political activity.

### Effective Date of Repeal

Pub. L. 93–203, title VI, § 614, Dec. 28, 1973, 87 Stat. 883, provided that the repeal by that section is effective with respect to fiscal years after June 30, 1974.
