# § 8601 to 8612. Repealed. Pub. L. 97–35, title XXVI, § 2611, Aug. 13, 1981, 95 Stat. 902

## Notes

### removalDescription

Section 8601, Pub. L. 96–223, title III, § 302, Apr. 2, 1980, 94 Stat. 288, set forth Congressional findings and declaration of purpose for low-income energy assistance program.

### removalDescription

Section 8602, Pub. L. 96–223, title III, § 303, Apr. 2, 1980, 94 Stat. 288, defined “household”, “home energy”, “lower living standard income level”, “Secretary”, and “State”.

### removalDescription

Section 8603, Pub. L. 96–223, title III, § 304, Apr. 2, 1980, 94 Stat. 289, related to authorizations for home energy grants.

### removalDescription

Section 8604, Pub. L. 96–223, title III, § 305, Apr. 2, 1980, 94 Stat. 289, set forth eligibility requirements for households.

### removalDescription

Section 8605, Pub. L. 96–223, title III, § 306, Apr. 2, 1980, 94 Stat. 289, set forth provisions respecting allotments for grants.

### removalDescription

Section 8606, Pub. L. 96–223, title III, § 307, Apr. 2, 1980, 94 Stat. 293, set forth limitations on uses of home energy grants for fiscal year 1981.

### removalDescription

Section 8607, Pub. L. 96–223, title III, § 308, Apr. 2, 1980, 94 Stat. 294, set forth provisions respecting submission, contents, etc., for State plans.

### removalDescription

Section 8608, Pub. L. 96–223, title III, § 309, Apr. 2, 1980, 94 Stat. 298, related to uniform collection data.

### removalDescription

Section 8609, Pub. L. 96–223, title III, § 310, Apr. 2, 1980, 94 Stat. 298, related to amount and methods of payment.

### removalDescription

Section 8610, Pub. L. 96–223, title III, § 311, Apr. 2, 1980, 94 Stat. 298, related to withholding of payments.

### removalDescription

Section 8611, Pub. L. 96–223, title III, § 312, Apr. 2, 1980, 94 Stat. 298, set forth criminal penalties for violations of provisions.

### removalDescription

Section 8612, Pub. L. 96–223, title III, § 313(a)–(c)(1), (d)–(g), Apr. 2, 1980, 94 Stat. 298, 299, related to administration and implementation of energy assistance programs.

### Effective Date of Repeal

Pub. L. 97–35, title XXVI, § 2611, Aug. 13, 1981, 95 Stat. 902, provided that the repeal made by that section is effective Oct. 1, 1981.

### Short Title

Pub. L. 96–223, title III, § 301, Apr. 2, 1980, 94 Stat. 288, provided that title III of Pub. L. 96–223, which enacted sections 8601 to 8612 of this title, and amended section 2014(d) of Title 7, Agriculture, was to be cited as the “Home Energy Assistance Act of 1980”, prior to repeal by Pub. L. 97–35, title XXVI, § 2611, Aug. 13, 1981, 95 Stat. 902.
