# § 401, 401a. Transferred

## Notes

### Codification

Sections 401 and 401a, comprising sections 2 and 3, respectively, of the National Security Act of 1947, act July 26, 1947, ch. 343, were editorially reclassified as sections 3002 and 3003, respectively, of chapter 44 of this title.
