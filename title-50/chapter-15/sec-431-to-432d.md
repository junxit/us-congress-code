# § 431 to 432d. Transferred

## Notes

### Codification

Sections 431 to 432d, comprising sections 701 to 706, respectively, of the National Security Act of 1947, act July 26, 1947, ch. 343, were editorially reclassified as sections 3141 to 3146, respectively, of chapter 44 of this title.
