# § 441 to 441d. Transferred

## Notes

### Codification

Sections 441 to 441d, comprising sections 901 to 905, respectively, of the National Security Act of 1947, act July 26, 1947, ch. 343, were editorially reclassified as sections 3171 to 3175, respectively, of chapter 44 of this title.
