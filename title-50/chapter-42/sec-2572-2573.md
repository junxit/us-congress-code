# § 2572, 2573. Repealed. Pub. L. 119–60, div. C, title XXXI, § 3111(b)(1), Dec. 18, 2025, 139 Stat. 1458

## Notes

### removalDescription

Section 2572, Pub. L. 107–314, div. D, title XLIII, § 4307, as added Pub. L. 110–181, div. C, title XXXI, § 3129(a)(1), Jan. 28, 2008, 122 Stat. 584, provided for international agreements on nuclear weapons data. See section 6157 of Title 10, Armed Forces.

### removalDescription

Section 2573, Pub. L. 107–314, div. D, title XLIII, § 4308, as added Pub. L. 110–181, div. C, title XXXI, § 3129(a)(1), Jan. 28, 2008, 122 Stat. 584, provided for international agreements on information on radioactive materials. See Section 6158 of Title 10, Armed Forces.
