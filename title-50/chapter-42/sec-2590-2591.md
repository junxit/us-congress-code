# § 2590, 2591. Repealed. Pub. L. 119–60, div. C, title XXXI, § 3111(b)(1), Dec. 18, 2025, 139 Stat. 1458

## Notes

### removalDescription

Section 2590, Pub. L. 107–314, div. D, title XLIV, § 4409, as added Pub. L. 116–92, div. C, title XXXI, § 3112(a), Dec. 20, 2019, 133 Stat. 1950, required annual submission of estimation of costs of meeting defense environmental cleanup milestones required by consent orders. See section 6181 of Title 10, Armed Forces.

### removalDescription

Section 2591, Pub. L. 107–314, div. D, title XLIV, § 4410, as added Pub. L. 116–283, div. C, title XXXI, § 3121(a), Jan. 1, 2021, 134 Stat. 4381, required a statement of environmental liabilities to be made available to the public each year. See section 6182 of Title 10, Armed Forces.
