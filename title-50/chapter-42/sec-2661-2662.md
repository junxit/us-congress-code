# § 2661, 2662. Repealed. Pub. L. 119–60, div. C, title XXXI, § 3111(b)(1), Dec. 18, 2025, 139 Stat. 1458

## Notes

### removalDescription

Section 2661, Pub. L. 107–314, div. D, title XLV, § 4510, as added Pub. L. 114–328, div. C, title XXXI, § 3112(a), Dec. 23, 2016, 130 Stat. 2756, authorized actions to protect certain nuclear facilities and assets from unmanned aircraft. See section 6227 of Title 10, Armed Forces.

### removalDescription

Section 2662, Pub. L. 107–314, div. D, title XLV, § 4511, as added Pub. L. 116–283, div. C, title XXXI, § 3131(a), Jan. 1, 2021, 134 Stat. 4383, required establishment of procedures for reporting penetrations of certain networks of contractors and subcontractors. See section 6228 of Title 10, Armed Forces.
