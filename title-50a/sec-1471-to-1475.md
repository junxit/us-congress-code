# § 1471 to 1475. Repealed. July 24, 1956, ch. 671, § 5(a)(3), 70 Stat. 606

## Notes

### removalDescription

Section 1471, acts June 23, 1943, ch. 142, § 1, 57 Stat. 162; Aug. 8, 1946, ch. 867, 60 Stat. 905; Aug. 8, 1946, ch. 912, 60 Stat. 945, authorized a certificate of service for service in merchant marine after May 1, 1940, and before the termination of the national emergency (July 25, 1947).

### removalDescription

Section 1472, acts June 23, 1943, ch. 142, § 2, 57 Stat. 162; Aug. 8, 1946, ch. 867, 60 Stat. 906, related to restoration of members of merchant marine to civilian employment.

### removalDescription

Section 1473, acts June 23, 1943, ch. 142, § 3, 57 Stat. 162; Aug. 8, 1946, ch. 867, 60 Stat. 907, related to proceedings to compel employer compliance with section 1472 of this Appendix.

### removalDescription

Section 1474, acts June 23, 1943, ch. 142, § 4, 57 Stat. 163; Aug. 8, 1946, ch. 867, 60 Stat. 907, related to additional compensation to certain members of merchant marine.

### removalDescription

Section 1475, acts June 23, 1943, ch. 142, § 5, 57 Stat. 163; Aug. 8, 1946, ch. 867, 60 Stat. 907, related to issuance of rules and regulations.

### Effective Date of Repeal

Repeal effective July 1, 1954, see section 5(a) of act July 24, 1956.
