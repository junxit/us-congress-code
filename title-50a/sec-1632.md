# § 1632. Repealed. June 30, 1949, ch. 288, title VI, § 602(a)(1), formerly title V, § 502(a)(1), 63 Stat. 399; renumbered title VI, § 602(a)(1), Sept. 5, 1950, ch. 849, § 6(a), (b), 64 Stat. 583

## Notes

### removalDescription

Section, acts Oct. 3, 1944, ch. 479, § 23, 58 Stat. 777; Sept. 18, 1945, ch. 368, § 2, 59 Stat. 533; Aug. 7, 1946, ch. 790, §§ 1, 2, 60 Stat. 886, related to disposal of surplus real property.

### Effective Date of Repeal

Repeal effective July 1, 1949, with priorities and preferences for surplus real estate continued until Dec. 31, 1949, see sections 602(a)(1) and 605, formerly sections 502(a)(1) and 505, of act June 30, 1949.
