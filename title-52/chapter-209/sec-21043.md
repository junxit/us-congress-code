# § 21043. Authorization of appropriations

- **(a)** *In general* There are authorized to be appropriated for grants under this subpart $20,000,000 for fiscal year 2003.
- **(b)** *Availability of funds* Amounts appropriated pursuant to the authorization under this section shall remain available, without fiscal year limitation, until expended.

## Source credit

(Pub. L. 107–252, title II, § 273, Oct. 29, 2002, 116 Stat. 1700.)
